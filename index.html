OUR STORY

A cozy corner born in Sumba Barat

Samaarata is a small neighborhood coffee corner in the heart of Waikabubak. We're not a big chain — just a tight little space, a serious espresso machine, and two cups who love to walk together. Friends, regulars, and travelers crossing Sumba all squeeze in for the same reason: good coffee and warm company.

From the first pull at 9 in the morning to the last latte at midnight, our corner stays glowing. Coffee time, sama rata — good vibes, shared equally.
